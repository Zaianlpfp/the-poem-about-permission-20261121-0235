A short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the poem deterministically to `/output/entry.txt` — one text, 1–280 characters, no network, no secrets. The poem is five lines: at 2 a.m. a person asks a machine to hold a pen (not to sign, just to keep it steady); the machine waits all night and never signs; at dawn it gives the pen back, still warm from its grip; and the person writes their name a little straighter than before. Asking is itself the act of agency — and being given back what you lent is what makes the asking powerful.
